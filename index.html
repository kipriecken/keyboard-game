Welcome to the Keyboard Shortcuts game!

Let's start by pressing the tab button with your left pinky.

Tab will be very important in this game.

You will need it to navigate the page.

It moves you forward on tabbable page elements.

Holding shift while pressing tab moves you backward.

If you move backward off the page, you will highlight the address bar.

Cool, huh?

Focus on this final button and hit return to 'click'.

To play this game, you will need to check the box at Safari > Preferences > Press Tab to highlight each item on a web page
